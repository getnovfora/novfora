# NovFora v1.3.0 is live — here's what's new

We've spent the last stretch making this place feel more like a home and less like a message board, and today it all goes live. Nothing you've posted has changed — same threads, same people, same history. What's changed is how much easier it is to talk to each other here.

Here's what's new, starting today.

## Multi-quote

Replying to more than one post used to mean copy-pasting your way into a mess. Now you can pull quotes from a whole basket of posts — even from different people — into one reply, each one properly attributed. Build the reply you actually meant to write.

## Follow forums and tags

Subscribing to a single thread was never quite enough. Now you can follow whole forums and tags, and everything you follow shows up in one place: your watched feed. Less digging, more of what you care about.

## A wall for your profile

Profiles got a real redesign — a tabbed view (Activity, Posts, About) with your member-since date, post count, reputation, and followers up top. And now you can actually post to your own wall: status updates, not just topic replies. It's starting to feel like *your* corner of the place.

## Announcements that actually reach you

Moderators and admins can now post dismissible announcement banners with real targeting — show them to everyone, or only where it matters. Close one once you've seen it and it stays closed.

## Faster moderation, right in the thread

Moderators can now approve or reject held replies without leaving the thread. Less back-and-forth in the mod queue, faster answers for everyone waiting on a post to clear.

## A warmer welcome for new members

New members now get a welcome email and a simple checklist to get oriented — make a first post, follow a forum, fill out a profile, set an avatar. Each step earns a badge. Small nudges, but they help.

## A cleaner front porch

The forum index, navigation, search, and thread view all got a pass — a card-based board layout that's easier to scan, more discoverable search, and a cleaner reading experience for threads, including clearer prompts for people who aren't signed in yet.

## Under the hood

A round of security and permission hardening, plus a batch of reliability fixes, went in alongside all of this. Nothing you'll notice day to day — that's the point.
