Features

A complete platform, in the box

NovFora 1.0 ships the fundamentals and the polish — permissions, editor, anti-spam, community, extensibility, and operations — with no paid add-ons to reach feature parity.

Forum & content

🗂️

Categories → forums → topics

A clear hierarchy with sub-forums, drag-and-drop structure, descriptions, and read-only areas.

✍️

WYSIWYG editor

Friendly by default, optional Markdown, BBCode for imports. Server-sanitized — always safe.

📎

Attachments & polls

Drag-drop uploads (EXIF-stripped, resized), inline polls, @mentions, and safe oEmbed.

🏷️

Prefixes & tags

Colored topic prefixes and cross-cutting tags keep busy forums scannable and findable.

💾

Drafts & edit history

Autosaved drafts so nothing's lost, full post revision history, and scheduled replies.

🔖

Bookmarks

Save any topic or post to a personal list to come back to later.

Permissions & roles

🔐

Allow / No / Never engine

Three-state permissions across every scope. Never is absolute — perfect for clean exceptions.

🔎

Permission inspector

Ask "why can (or can't) this person do that?" and see the decisive rule and every entry.

👥

Groups & auto-promotion

Membership models, AND/OR auto-promotion by participation, and a custom role builder.

🛡️

Per-forum moderators

Hand a forum to a trusted regular with scoped, capability-bundled moderation powers.

👑

Co-owners & restricted admins

Multiple owners with a last-owner guard, or grant someone just part of the admin panel.

⏱️

Temporary delegation

Lend one capability for a bounded window — it expires on its own, no cleanup to remember.

Community & social

❤️

Reactions & reputation

Lightweight reactions that build reputation, plus achievement badges for milestones.

👤

Profiles & custom fields

Rich profiles with admin-defined custom fields, signatures, and activity.

✉️

Private messages

Multi-participant, threaded conversations with the same safe editor as posts.

🏛️

Clubs

Member-run sub-communities with their own forums and public / closed / private privacy.

📇

Members directory

An opt-in directory and a reputation leaderboard, with follow and ignore controls.

📈

Activity & trending

What's-new feeds for what you follow, and permission-safe trending topics.

Moderation & anti-spam

✅

Queue & reports

Approve held content and resolve member reports from a dedicated moderator panel.

⚖️

Warnings & infractions

Graduated, points-based warnings that decay and escalate consequences automatically.

🧹

Bans & spam cleaner

Reversible bans, plus one-click ban-and-remove-all-content for obvious spammers.

📊

Trust levels

Five levels that grow with good participation and gate capabilities and post holds.

🤖

StopForumSpam & CAPTCHA

Crowd blocklist screening, swappable CAPTCHA (Turnstile), honeypot, and rate limits.

🗒️

Staff notes & word filters

Private staff notes on members and server-side word filters with replace / flag / block.

Platform & operations

🔌

Modules, API & webhooks

A versioned, no-core-edit module API, a permission-aware REST API, and signed webhooks.

🔑

SSO & 2FA

Google / GitHub / Discord sign-in, mandatory staff 2FA, and an enterprise SAML scaffold.

📦

Importers

Resumable phpBB / XenForo / MyBB / SMF imports with passwords and 301 redirects preserved.

🎨

Theming & layout

Point-and-click appearance, a layout configurator, and a sandboxed template editor.

⬆️

Installer & upgrades

No-SSH web installer, reversible migrations, backup-first auto-upgrade, and panel backups.

🌍

PWA, push & i18n

Installable PWA with web push, full notifications, search, analytics, and RTL localization.
