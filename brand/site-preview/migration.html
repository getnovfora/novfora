Migration

Bring your community with you

Don't start from zero. NovFora imports members, forums, topics, posts, and attachments — with passwords preserved and your old URLs redirected — so the move keeps your people and your search rankings.

What comes across

Your community, intact

👥

Members & passwords

Accounts come over with hashed passwords preserved — people keep their logins (a reset is prompted only if the old hash format differs).

🗂️

Forums & content

Your category/forum structure, every topic and post — old BBCode translated to NovFora's clean format.

📎

Attachments, verified

Files come across and are checksum-verified, so nothing arrives corrupted or missing.

🔗

SEO 301 redirects

Old URLs map to 301 redirects, so existing links and your search rankings survive the move.

🔁

Resumable & idempotent

Interrupted? Re-run it — the importer picks up where it left off and never duplicates.

🤫

Quiet by design

Importing fires no notifications, reputation awards, or feed spam — members aren't blasted on day one.

How it runs

Safe on real, large forums

Preflight

A read-only pass counts everything and plans the work, so you know the full scope before committing.

Import

Users, forums, topics, posts, and attachments come over in batches, tracked entity by entity.

Verify

Row counts and content are checked — including attachment checksums — to confirm a faithful copy.

Recommended cutover

A calm switch-over plan

  • Stand up NovFora on your target host with registration closed.
  • Back up both the source forum and the fresh install.
  • Preflight, then import from the admin panel (or php artisan novfora:import).
  • Verify — spot-check members, big topics, and attachments; confirm old URLs redirect.
  • Rebuild search, review permissions, and set up email.
  • Open registration, announce the move, and keep the old site read-only for a while.

NovFora reads a source forum's database only to import your data — it never copies the other software. Your content moves; you're running NovFora.
