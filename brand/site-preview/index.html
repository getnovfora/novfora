Why NovFora

Forums fail for the same handful of reasons

We studied what kills communities on phpBB, MyBB, SMF, XenForo, and Discourse — then fixed each one in the core, not as an afterthought.

🛡️

Spam overwhelms

Spam is the number-one forum killer, usually bolted on late.

✓ Trust levels wired into permissions, blocklist screening, swappable CAPTCHA, rate limits, and a hold queue — first-class from day one.
🔍

Search is weak

Members can't find what they need, so knowledge rots.

✓ MySQL full-text on shared hosting, Meilisearch when you scale — one experience, always permission-safe.
📱

Mobile feels dated

Most forums still feel like 2009 on a phone.

✓ A mobile-first interface and an installable PWA with push notifications.
🧩

Add-ons break upgrades

One plugin and you're stuck on an old version forever.

✓ A versioned module API with pre-upgrade compatibility checks — add-ons that survive upgrades.
🎨

Theming needs core edits

Customize the look and the next update wipes it out.

✓ Point-and-click appearance, a layout configurator, and a sandboxed template layer — no core edits, ever.
📦

Migration is fragile

Moving in loses attachments, passwords, and your SEO.

✓ Resumable importers with attachment verification, password preservation, and 301 redirect maps.
Everything, in the box

A complete community platform

NovFora 1.0 ships the fundamentals and the polish — no paid add-ons to reach feature parity.

🔐

Permissions you can reason about

A three-state Allow / No / Never engine across categories, forums, and topics — with an inspector that tells you exactly why anyone can or can't do anything.

✍️

WYSIWYG-first editor

Friendly by default, Markdown when you want it, BBCode for imports. Attachments, polls, mentions, and oEmbed built in.

🛡️

First-class anti-spam

Trust levels, StopForumSpam, CAPTCHA, rate limits, word filters, and a false-positive-guarded hold queue.

👥

Clubs & groups

Member-run sub-communities with their own forums and privacy, plus auto-promoting groups and custom roles.

🔑

SSO & accounts

Sign in with Google, GitHub, or Discord; mandatory 2FA for staff; an enterprise SAML scaffold.

⚙️

REST API & webhooks

A versioned, permission-aware API and HMAC-signed, SSRF-guarded webhooks to wire NovFora into anything.

⬆️

Upgrades that don't break

Reversible migrations and a backup-first, cron-driven auto-upgrade. No manual database surgery.

📲

PWA & notifications

Installable on any device, with web push, digests, and a full in-app notification system.

💳

Paid memberships

Membership tiers with perks gated cleanly through the permission engine — manual or Stripe, your call.

The permission engine

Know exactly who can do what — and why

Most forums make permissions a guessing game. NovFora gives you phpBB-grade control with something none of them have: an inspector that traces every verdict.

◆
Three states, every scope. Allow, No, or Never across categories → forums → topics. Never is absolute.
◆
Groups, roles & trust, merged. Individual, group, and trust-level grants resolve predictably.
◆
The inspector. Ask "can this person do that here?" and see the decisive rule and every contributing entry.
